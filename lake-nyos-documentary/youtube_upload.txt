TITLE
The Lake That Killed 1,700 People Without Drowning Them

DESCRIPTION
On the night of 21 August 1986, Lake Nyos in Cameroon released a huge cloud of carbon dioxide. At least 1,700 people died in their homes and villages, and not one of them drowned. This is the story of what happened, why it was so deadly, and how scientists stopped it from happening again.
