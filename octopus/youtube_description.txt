The Octopus: An Alien Mind on Earth

Three hearts. Blue blood. Arms that think for themselves. A skin that changes colour in a blink, and may even sense light. The octopus took a completely different road to intelligence than we did, and the result is the closest thing to an alien we will ever meet.
