How a Dead Man Fooled Hitler — Operation Mincemeat (1943)

In April 1943, British intelligence dressed a dead man as a Royal Marines officer, chained a briefcase of fake invasion plans to him, and let the sea carry him to the coast of Spain. This is the true story of the deception that helped open the door to Europe.
