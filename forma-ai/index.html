    About trust, how Forma fits your workflow, and working together.

    Trust

    No. Your briefs, research, and designs are never used to train Forma’s models or any third-party model. On Team and Enterprise plans, admins also choose how long uploaded research is kept.

    Only from the sources you attach to a project, such as PRDs, interview notes, tickets, and analytics exports. When Forma makes a decision none of your sources support, it labels it as an assumption so you can add evidence or change it.

    You do. Designs, flows, prototypes, and exports belong to you or your organization, on every plan, including Starter.

    Workflow

    No. Forma starts with a neutral component library. When you connect a Figma library on Pro or Team, new and existing screens can be rebuilt with your components and tokens.

    Yes. Export frames to Figma as editable layers with auto layout and real component instances. Developers get specs and design tokens for each screen.

    Edit it directly on the canvas, or select it and describe the change. Forma only regenerates what you select, and every version is kept in history so you can compare or roll back.

    Collaboration

    Comment on any element, request a review from a person or a group such as your design systems team, and approve or request changes. Reviews and their outcomes stay attached to the design.

    Only people who edit designs. On Team, anyone can view, comment, and click through prototypes for free.
