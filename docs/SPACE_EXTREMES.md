# 7 Things in Space So Extreme They Shouldn't Exist

A fast-paced countdown in a "Be Amazed"-style format. 854×480, 30 fps, 5:14. Built in Remotion (`space/`).

## Titles
1. **7 Things in Space So Extreme They Shouldn't Exist** ← selected. It promises a ranked payoff and sets up a contrast: things that seem impossible, but are real and measured.
2. The Most Extreme Things in the Universe, Ranked
3. Space Is Way Weirder Than You Think
4. 7 Space Facts That Break Your Sense of Scale
5. Earth Fits Inside This Storm… and It's Only #5

## Thumbnail concepts (max 3 words)
- **"EARTH FITS INSIDE"**: Jupiter's Great Red Spot filling the frame with a small Earth inside it, and an orange arrow pointing at Earth.
- **"BIGGER THAN EVERYTHING"**: a glowing black hole with the solar system's orbits drawn inside its dark circle.

## Engagement design
- **Hook (0–8 s):** a concrete wow ("Earth would fit inside it"), immediately topped by "only number five", which opens a loop that lasts the whole video.
- **Countdown structure:** every entry opens on a 2-second sting (warp streaks, a flash, the number slamming in, a whoosh and boom) and then lands a number within about 3 seconds.
- **Every entry has a twist:** cliffs at the edges, still there decades later, it's shrinking, a cosmic lighthouse, it reached Earth from across the galaxy, room to spare, brighter than expected.
- **Kinetic captions:** words pop in as they're spoken, with numbers and keywords in orange, so it works with the sound off.
- **Nothing static:** a 3-layer parallax starfield, a constant camera push or orbit, and every object animated (bands flow, storms swirl, beams sweep, discs spin). Real images appear only as moving "REAL IMAGE" proof inserts.
