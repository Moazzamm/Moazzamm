# Script framework

The script is what makes a video premium. Visuals and sound amplify a good script; they can't rescue a weak one.

## 1. Before writing: the one-line promise

Write one sentence: *"By the end, the viewer will ___."* Every line in the script must serve it. If a line doesn't, cut it.

Also write down:
- **Viewer**: who is watching (for example "Pakistani diaspora planning a trip home" or "budget backpackers").
- **Curiosity gap**: the question the title and thumbnail raise. The video answers it, and only near the end.
- **Payoff**: the moment the viewer is waiting for. Put it in the last third.

## 2. Hook (0–30 s): earn the next 30 seconds

Pick one formula. Never open with "hey guys", the channel name, or a recap.

| Formula | Travel example | Faceless example |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold open on the peak moment**, then "but 3 days ago…" | Standing at the edge of the glacier, wind howling → rewind | The ship's final radio message → rewind |
| **Contradiction** | "This is the cheapest country I've visited, and I've never eaten better." | "The bluest lake in Asia was created by a disaster." |
| **Stakes / problem** | "We have 12 hours to cross this pass before the road closes for winter." | "In 1978 an entire village vanished overnight." |
| **Specific number** | "I spent $412 for 7 days in Hunza. Here's every rupee." | "Only 3 people have ever reached this place." |
| **Challenge / question** | "Can you really travel the Karakoram Highway by local bus?" | "Why does nobody live on the world's most fertile island?" |

Hook rules:
- The first sentence is under 12 words.
- Show what you say. The visual proves the claim within 3 seconds.
- Open a loop you close later ("…and what we found at the top changed the whole trip").
- End the hook with a **promise line**: "In this video I'll show you X, Y, and the one thing nobody tells you about Z."
- Then cut to the title card.

## 3. Retention structure (the body)

Use **escalating beats**. Each beat is a mini-story: *setup → tension → payoff → bridge*.

```
HOOK (0:00–0:30)       peak moment / contradiction + promise
TITLE CARD             ~0:10–0:30
BEAT 1                 easiest win: arrival, first impression, first fact
BEAT 2                 complication: something goes wrong, surprises
  ↳ re-hook (~35% mark) "But that wasn't the strangest part…"
BEAT 3                 deepest / most emotional / most surprising
BEAT 4 (PAYOFF)        close the opening loop: the summit, the answer, the reveal
OUTRO (≤ 20 s)         one-line takeaway + specific CTA to the next video
END SCREEN (20 s)
```

Retention devices (use at least one every 60–90 s):
- **Open loops**: "Remember that police checkpoint? That becomes important."
- **Pattern interrupts**: a cut to a stat counter, map, kinetic text, a hard music stop, or silence.
- **But / therefore** instead of "and then". Each beat should *cause* the next one.
- **Specifics beat adjectives**: "−14 °C at 4,700 m" instead of "really cold and high".
- **Re-hook before the midpoint.** People drop at about 30–40%, so tease the payoff there.

Never: a long intro, "before we start, subscribe", a sponsor in the first 60 s, or an outro longer than 20 s.

## 4. Vlog structure (Moazzam Travels)

Footage is already shot, so the "script" is the edit order plus voiceover patches.
1. Log every clip: moment, emotion, any line worth using.
2. Find the **peak moment** (best view, biggest reaction, funniest fail). It becomes the cold open.
3. Order the beats: travel there (route map) → arrival (title + location tag) → experiences ranked by intensity → payoff → reflection.
4. Where footage lacks context, write **VO patches** (short, ≤ 2 sentences) for Moazzam to record on his phone. Treat these as script and give him the exact lines.
5. Practical info (cost, transport, time, tips) goes in on-screen stat counters, not long talking segments.

## 5. Faceless script format

```
## HOOK
[VISUAL: aerial of turquoise lake, slow push-in]
In 2010, a mountain fell into a river.
[VISUAL: kinetic-text "DROWNED A HIGHWAY"] [SFX: impact_boom]
It drowned a highway, swallowed three villages, and created the bluest lake in Asia.
...
```

- 140–160 spoken words per minute. A 10-minute video is about 1,500 words.
- Paragraphs of 1–3 sentences. Each paragraph gets a `[VISUAL]` cue.
- Mark punchlines that become kinetic-text cards with `[KT: …]`.

## 6. Writing for the ear (TTS and Moazzam's VO)

- Short sentences. Vary the length: short, short, longer one that flows, then a punch.
- Spell numbers the way they're spoken: "four thousand seven hundred metres", "twenty-ten" or "two thousand ten" (pick one).
- Avoid parentheses, slashes, abbreviations and emoji. Kokoro reads them literally or awkwardly.
- Use commas and full stops to control Kokoro's pacing. Use "…" for a dramatic pause and a new paragraph for a longer one.
- Read every line out loud (in your head). If you'd stumble, rewrite it.

## 7. Self-review checklist (run before showing Moazzam)

- [ ] The first sentence is under 12 words and hooks without context.
- [ ] The promise line is in the first 30 s.
- [ ] There is at least one open loop, closed in the final third.
- [ ] There's a re-hook around the 35% mark.
- [ ] No paragraph without a visual cue (faceless).
- [ ] Every fact has a source in `notes.md`.
- [ ] Outro ≤ 20 s, CTA names a specific next video.
- [ ] 3 title options (≤ 60 chars) + 3 thumbnail texts (≤ 4 words) that match the hook's curiosity gap.
