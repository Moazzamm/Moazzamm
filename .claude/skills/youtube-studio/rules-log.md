# Rules log

Every lesson from Moazzam's feedback, newest last. These override the reference files.
Format: `R<n> (<date>, <episode>): <rule — specific and testable>`

## Starting rules

- R1 (setup): The first 5 seconds must show the most visually striking shot or the most surprising line — never a greeting, logo or "hey guys".
- R2 (setup): Title card comes AFTER the hook (around 0:08–0:20), never at 0:00.
- R3 (setup): SFX are seasoning: at most one SFX per 2 seconds, and never under a sentence that carries information.
- R4 (setup): Music never competes with voice; if a word is hard to hear, music comes down, not voice up.
- R5 (setup): Every on-screen number he speaks gets a stat counter or kinetic text.
- R6 (setup): Keep the last clean take of any repeated line; cut breaths longer than 0.4 s between sentences.
